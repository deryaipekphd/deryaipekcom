How to read the timing

  • The critical path is the chain with the least slack against an eight-semester finish. A course is on it when it sets the earliest date of the next one.
  • Earliest semesters follow prerequisites and offering terms only. Credit load, summer and winter terms, waivers and transfer credit are ignored.
  • Irregular and unlisted offerings are drawn as if they ran every semester.
  • Junior standing is counted as semester 5 and sophomore standing as semester 3. CSC 356 follows the soft rule of semester 5 on.
  • Electives are drawn but do not enter the critical path.
  • Course summaries are condensed from the 2026-27 catalog listings, read on 23 November 2026. They are not the catalog's wording; each links to the full entry.
